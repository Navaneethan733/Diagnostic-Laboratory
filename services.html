Preparation Guide

Pre-Test Fasting Guidelines

Simple guidelines to ensure your test sample yields 100% accurate, undistorted clinical results.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of plain water before your blood draw. Good hydration makes vein location effortless and maintains normal blood viscosity.

⏳ Fasting Hours

For Fasting Blood Glucose and Lipid Profiles, abstain from food and tea/coffee for 10 to 12 hours prior to sample collection.

💊 Medications

Continue taking prescribed chronic medications with water unless specifically advised otherwise by your primary doctor.
